EST to IST Time Converter

India Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30) is 10 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Eastern Standard Time (EST, UTC-5). When it is 12:00 PM EST, it is 10:30 PM IST on the same day.

Daylight Saving Time (DST) Note: When US Eastern Time observes Daylight Saving Time (EDT, UTC-4, typically March to November), IST is 9 hours and 30 minutes ahead.

Meeting & Remote Work Overlap

The easiest overlap window for US East Coast and India teams is 8:00 AM – 11:30 AM EST, which equals 6:30 PM – 10:00 PM IST (during standard time).

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours ahead is IST from EST?
IST is 10 hours and 30 minutes ahead of EST (UTC-5), and 9 hours and 30 minutes ahead during US Daylight Saving Time (EDT, UTC-4).

What is 9 AM EST in IST?
9:00 AM EST is 7:30 PM IST on the same day during standard time.
