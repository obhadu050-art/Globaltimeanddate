Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about time zones, conversions, or scheduling meetings? Find the answers below.

Q: How does Global Time and Date handle Daylight Saving Time (DST)?

A: Our system dynamically factors in official regional time zone databases. Daylight Saving Time adjustments are applied automatically based on geographical locations and current dates.

Q: Is Global Time and Date free for personal and commercial use?

A: Yes, Global Time and Date is 100% free with unlimited location tracking, visual time comparison, and meeting scheduling tools.

Q: How do I find the best meeting slot for multi-continent remote teams?

A: Add all team member locations to the grid. Look for column slots where all locations show green working-hour tiles (9 AM – 5 PM local time).

Q: Can I use this tool on my mobile phone or desktop like an app?

A: Absolutely! We support Progressive Web App (PWA) features. Just click the "Install App" button on the main converter page to save it directly to your home screen or desktop.

Q: How do I switch between 12-hour and 24-hour time formats?

A: Simply click the "12h / 24h" toggle button located right above the time grid to switch formats instantly across all rows.
