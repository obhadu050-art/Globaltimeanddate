How to Use Global Time and Date Converter

Mastering cross-border scheduling and international time zone conversion is simple with our visual 24-hour interactive grid.

Step 1: Add Your Target Locations

Use the top search input bar to type the name of any major city, country, or specific time zone code (e.g., New York, London, Tokyo, PST, EST, or GMT). Click + Add Location to include it in your visual comparison grid.

Step 2: Compare Hours Using the Interactive Tile Grid

Every added location displays a full 24-hour horizontal tile slider. Click or tap any specific hour block on any city row. All other connected cities will instantly shift and align to display their exact local time at that exact hour.

Step 3: Identify Optimal Business Working Hours

Finding meeting times across global teams is effortless. Tiles highlighted in green represent standard business working hours (9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time). Look for vertical columns where all participants' cities show green tiles for seamless scheduling.

Step 4: Toggle 12-Hour / 24-Hour Displays

Need military time format or standard AM/PM format? Click the 12h / 24h toggle button above the converter to switch display preferences across all rows in real time.

Step 5: Install as a Home Screen App (PWA)

For instant access without searching online every time, click the green 📲 Install App button at the top of the converter card to save Global Time and Date directly onto your desktop or mobile phone home screen.
